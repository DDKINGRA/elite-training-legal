Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2025

Elite Training ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personally identifiable information:

  • Name and email address - for account creation and authentication
  • Profile photos - uploaded by users to personalize their accounts
  • Body measurements, weight, and fitness progress data - to track your fitness journey
  • Workout history, exercise logs, and nutrition records - to provide training services
  • Chat messages between clients and coaches - to enable communication
  • Device information and push notification tokens - for delivering app notifications
  • Usage data and app interaction statistics - to improve user experience

Camera Permission

Our App requests access to your device's camera. This permission is used solely for the following purpose:

  • Profile Photo Upload: Users can take photos with their camera to set or update their profile picture within the App. This feature allows clients and coaches to personalize their accounts with a visual identity.

We do not use the camera for any other purpose. Photos taken are stored securely and are only visible within the App to authorized users (your coach or clients, as applicable). We do not share your photos with third parties for advertising or any other purposes.

How We Use Your Information

This information is collected solely to:

  • Provide fitness training services
  • Enable communication between coaches and clients
  • Improve the user experience within the App
  • Send push notifications about scheduled sessions and messages

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App or at our support email.
